**Badge** — a compliance / certification pill for legal identifiers and trust signals.

```jsx
<Badge label="RCCM" value="RB/COT/24 B 12345" />
<Badge label="IFU" value="3201900000000" variant="gold" />
<Badge label="Membre CCI Bénin" variant="neutral" icon={null} />
```

Use `cert` (green) for primary registration numbers, `gold` for distinctions, `neutral` for memberships. Numbers render in tabular figures. Keep these in the header lockup or footer — they're trust signals, not decoration.
